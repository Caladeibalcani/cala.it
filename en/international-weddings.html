Destination Wedding in Salento

Imagine celebrating your wedding at a sea-view venue in Salento, where the blue of the Adriatic meets the history and the authenticity of Puglia.

Cala dei Balcani, in Santa Cesarea Terme, is a sea-view wedding venue in Puglia set in Mediterranean nature, among dry-stone walls, a historic 16th-century Saracen tower, a pine wood and terraces overlooking the clear sea.

Every space tells the essence of Salento: stone rooms with their distinctive vaults, Mediterranean scents, breathtaking views and a kitchen built on local flavours and local produce.

An exclusive setting for those dreaming of an authentic, refined wedding in Salento — and for couples living far away who want to plan a destination wedding in Puglia with the sea at its heart.

From the ceremony to the reception, every moment can take place in a different setting, leading the couple and their guests through the property and the beauty around it.

Cala dei Balcani is not simply a wedding venue: it is an experience in the heart of Salento, suspended between history, nature and the sea.

Frequently Asked Questions

International Weddings: Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose Salento, in Puglia, for an international wedding?

Salento offers landscapes you find nowhere else, beautiful beaches, living traditions and a celebrated cuisine, alongside the infrastructure and services international visitors expect. It is full immersion in Apulian culture — ideal for both a wedding and a holiday nobody forgets.

Is your venue in Santa Cesarea Terme also a registered place for civil ceremonies?

Yes. Civil ceremonies can be held here, which means couples marry legally in a beautiful, authentic setting without moving anywhere else — and the paperwork is simpler for it.

Can a foreign couple marry in Salento without legal complications?

Certainly. We work with specialists who handle the international legal side. We help you obtain the documents, the official translations and the permits, and stay with you at every step so everything complies with Italian and international law.

Do you have an in-house wedding planner?

Yes. A dedicated professional planner guides you through every stage: choosing suppliers, decorations and which local traditions to include, through to shaping the day around you. The planner coordinates every detail.

What are the main services your Salento venue offers?
  • Civil or symbolic ceremonies, including those held in the registered room on site
  • Support from a professional wedding planner
  • Exceptional reception spaces, indoors and outdoors
  • Catering with local and international cuisine, allowing for allergies and specific dietary needs
  • Luxury accommodation and travel packages for international guests
  • Bespoke decorations and themed styling
  • International logistical and administrative support
  • Photography, video and entertainment services
How does a wedding ceremony work in Salento?

You can choose a civil ceremony in our registered room, a religious ceremony in a historic church, or a symbolic ceremony anywhere on the property.

How is accommodation arranged for international guests?

We work with hotels, villas and farm stays in Santa Cesarea Terme, with tailored packages and transfers. We also arrange tours and excursions built around the natural and cultural heritage of Salento.

Is the venue easy to reach, and do you arrange transport?

The venue is about 200 km from Bari, 80 km from Brindisi and 40 km from Lecce railway station. We arrange private transfers, shuttles and packages to make the journey easy.

Is there staff on site who speak English, French or German?

Yes — our team can look after you in English, French or German.

Will we have one multilingual contact (a wedding planner) from start to finish, across the distance and the language barrier?

Absolutely. Our promise is zero stress: you are assigned a multilingual planner (English, French or German) who runs the planning with you. With regular video calls and open communication, every detail is handled properly even from thousands of kilometres away.

How do you ensure safety and privacy during the ceremony?

The property offers private spaces, qualified staff and full compliance with safety rules. Protecting the privacy of the couple and their guests in an exclusive setting is our first priority.

How is the catering organised?

We offer a varied menu: traditional Salento dishes, international specialities and dedicated menus for allergies or specific diets. Our kitchen is built on fresh fish, local produce, regional wines and handmade desserts.

Can the venue host events before and after the wedding?

Yes. We organise brunches, rehearsal dinners and post-wedding dinners, so the whole stay becomes part of the celebration.

What does it cost roughly, and how is a quote prepared?

The price depends on the season, the size of the event, extra services and how much you customise. Quotes are transparent, with no surprises: prices start at around €130 per person for events of about 100 guests.

Do you host weddings with fewer than 100 guests?

Certainly. We have always hosted weddings of 30, 40, 50 or 60 people, and generally under 100 guests: we call them intimate weddings, and we genuinely believe in these smaller, more private celebrations.

Is it possible to hold an eco-friendly wedding in Salento?

Yes. We offer eco-compatible services, organic catering, sustainable materials and natural decorations, in line with responsible tourism.

Do you offer hair and make-up on site?

Yes. We work with professionals who come to the venue or to your hotel for hair, make-up and styling, so you do not have to go anywhere.

What are the emerging wedding trends in Salento?

Boho-chic ceremonies, outdoor civil ceremonies with minimal styling, menus built on local produce, and experience-led weddings drawing on Apulian culture and tradition.

How can we weave cultural and traditional elements into our wedding?

With our planner you can bring in music, dance, local rituals, food and wine, creating something authentic that your international guests will remember.

Are pets welcome?

Yes, the venue is pet-friendly, so your four-legged family can be part of the day. We can arrange pet-friendly ceremonies with dedicated space and care for their wellbeing.

Where exactly is the venue, and why is its position so good?

We are in Santa Cesarea Terme, known for its thermal baths, natural springs and coastline. The position gives easy access to the airports of Bari, Brindisi and Lecce, and direct access to beaches, nature reserves and well-known sights. It is the right base for pairing a wedding with a holiday in Salento.

What does Santa Cesarea Terme offer international visitors?

Santa Cesarea Terme is a well-established destination with good infrastructure, transfer services and plenty to do: spas, natural thermal springs, restaurants, boat trips and an old town full of character. Comfort, slow days and something genuine — which is what visitors from anywhere come for.

Can you organise an all-inclusive wedding in Santa Cesarea Terme?

Certainly. Our experience lets us put everything together quickly and without stress, shaped around what international couples actually need.

Are you open all year round?

Yes, we are open all year. Weddings work here in summer, in autumn and in winter alike — the setting stays elegant and comfortable through the seasons.

What is the weather like, and what is your plan B if it turns?

Puglia has a Mediterranean climate made for weddings, with long sunny days. Even so, we keep an indoor plan B that can take the whole event in comfort and style, without losing the feeling of the day.

Are there hidden costs or extras on top of the wedding price?

None at all. We work on complete financial transparency. You get a detailed quote covering every service you chose: nothing is hidden. Any additional cost is documented and submitted for your approval first, so you stay in control throughout.

What is the food philosophy at Cala dei Balcani?

Our philosophy celebrates the food and wine traditions of Puglia and Salento, with impeccably fresh ingredients. We are flexible about including international dishes and adapting menus (vegan, halal, kosher), and we pay close attention to intolerances and allergies so every guest eats safely and well.
