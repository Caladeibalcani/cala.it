A Wedding That Lasts Longer Than a Day

A wedding that turns into a holiday

One of the real advantages of marrying in Santa Cesarea Terme is that the wedding does not stop at the ceremony day.

The couple and their guests can make a whole experience of it, turning the stay into a real holiday in the heart of Salento.

Clear sea, walks along the seafront, restaurants, bars, excursions and towns to visit frame a wedding that carries on well past the reception.

A Short Walk from the Venue

Plenty of options, a short walk from the venue

Santa Cesarea Terme has a wide choice of hotels, resorts, B&Bs, holiday homes and apartments, for every need and budget.

Many places to stay are a few minutes away, and several are a short walk from Cala dei Balcani, so guests reach the venue easily and spend the day without long transfers.

For the couple and their guests that means more comfort, more freedom, and the chance to enjoy every moment without worrying.

Beyond the Reception

The wedding at the centre of something larger

Unlike many venues set inland, Cala dei Balcani sits inside one of the most appealing towns in Salento.

Guests can walk the seafront, stop at a bar overlooking the water, spend time on the beach, visit the historic thermal baths or explore the coast before and after the wedding.

The reception becomes the centrepiece of something much broader.
