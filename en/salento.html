Cala dei Balcani is Salento

Kissed by the sun, cradled by the sea and brushed by a light breeze, Salento is one of the most loved corners of Italy. At the very bottom of the peninsula, this long flat strip of land touches two seas: the Adriatic to the east, the Ionian to the west.

Santa Cesarea Terme

The Clear Sea of Santa Cesarea Terme

Santa Cesarea Terme is also beaches and clear water to dive into on hot summer days: rocky coves set between tall limestone cliffs, with water of an extraordinary blue. And when the sun goes down the sea keeps going: on full-moon nights the silver trail reaches right under the terrace.

Choosing to marry in Salento means giving yourselves and your guests a holiday day in one of the most beautiful regions anywhere.

Cala dei Balcani holds all of it: a place where nature, history and beauty come together.

Towns Worth Visiting

People coming to your wedding are not coming for a day. From Cala dei Balcani some of the finest places in Puglia are a short drive away — one more reason for your guests to stay a little longer.

Castro

About 10 minutes by car

The harbour, the sea caves and the Zinzulusa: the nearest town, the one where guests go down for a swim.

Otranto

About 25 minutes by car

The easternmost town in Italy: the walled old town, the cathedral with its Tree of Life mosaic, and turquoise water.

Santa Maria di Leuca

About 45 minutes by car

De Finibus Terrae, where the Adriatic meets the Ionian: the lighthouse, the sanctuary and the 19th-century villas along the front.

Lecce

About an hour by car

The capital of Baroque: Santa Croce, Piazza Duomo and the Roman amphitheatre, all in the golden stone of the Salento quarries.

Gallipoli

About an hour and a quarter by car

On the other coast, the Ionian one: the old town on its island, white sand beaches and sunsets over the water.
