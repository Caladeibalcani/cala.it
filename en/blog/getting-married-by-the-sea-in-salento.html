Getting Married by the Sea in Salento: This Is Cala dei Balcani

Home›Blog›Getting Married by the Sea in Salento
← Back to the blog
Venue & Style · 27 March 2024 · Cala dei Balcani

Cala dei Balcani looks straight out over the Salento sea, with a view that runs from Cala di Leuca to the Balkans. With the Saracen tower behind them, many of our couples choose to hold their civil ceremony facing that open water. It stays with the couple and with the guests.

Beyond the view, we have a particular idea of what a wedding should be. It comes from Francesco Urso, the owner, who wants your day to be anything but routine. If you want a wedding where everyone sits down and eats, this is the wrong place. If you want one that moves, that involves people, that feels like yours, it is the right one.

A Wedding That Moves

Here, a wedding that is genuinely different stops being an idea. We put entertainment, enjoyment and people talking to each other at the centre, and our in-house team gives every detail the attention it needs.

The property has enough corners for guests to move through it and take the place in. From the ceremony to the end of the reception, everything is arranged so that people are part of it rather than watching it.

Picture saying your vows with the sea behind you, while your family and friends look past you to the horizon. The view from the tower, from Cala di Leuca to the Balkans, does a lot of the work on its own.

One Wedding at a Time

Your day deserves undivided attention, so we only do exclusive weddings. The staff on duty are working on your wedding and nothing else. From the moment you arrive, you are treated as family rather than as clients.

Our own kitchen cooks everything on site, with fresh ingredients and real skill. The chefs build the menu around what you like, and you are free to change any part of it.

Our maître stays with you throughout, taking you and your guests through the spaces: the Saracen Tower, the Panoramic Court, the Cathedral Hall, the Belvedere Terrace.

Our Own Team, Not Contractors

Three things sit under everything we do: family, freedom and enthusiasm. We want every couple looked after, and every guest down to the last one to feel at ease. Each member of the team is chosen for care, warmth and the ability to listen.

We are here to do what you have in mind, and to shape every part of the day around it — from the decoration to the entertainment. Moving and amusing your guests is the part we enjoy most.

Your Wedding in Salento

If you are looking for a wedding in Salento that people remember, this is the place. The exclusivity, the in-house team, the kitchen and the spaces are what make it work — above the sea, on the day that matters most.
