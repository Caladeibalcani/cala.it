Planning a Salento Wedding From Abroad: Cristina and Matteo’s Experience

Home›Blog›Planning From Abroad: Cristina and Matteo
← Back to the blog
Love Stories·18 November 2024·Cala dei Balcani

Planning a wedding is demanding enough. Doing it from far away, without being there for every site visit, every tasting, every detail, can look impossible. Cristina and Matteo show otherwise: with the right team beside you, a Salento wedding works even from thousands of kilometres away.

“When the organisation is clear, the team is reliable and the approach feels like family, the result goes beyond what you expected.”

Before the Wedding: the Part Nobody Dreads

Planning at a distance became manageable because Cala dei Balcani stayed present. Cristina and Matteo felt understood from the first message — video calls, detailed advice and a team that anticipated their worries built real trust. They never felt left alone with it.

01.
First Contact, Shared VisionFrom the first message the team understood what the couple wanted. That turned the relationship from professional to personal almost at once.
02.
Video Calls and Guided AdviceEvery question was settled on a call. Virtual tours of the property, tastings coordinated remotely, supplier presentations: all of it handled with the right tools and real attention.
03.
Constant UpdatesThe team kept Cristina and Matteo informed at every stage — no surprises, no worry left hanging. A steady presence that made up for the distance.

The Day Itself: as if They Were at Home

When they finally arrived in Santa Cesarea Terme, Cristina and Matteo found exactly what they had pictured — and more. The warmth of the staff, the scale of the spaces and the care taken over everything made them feel like guests of a family rather than clients of a venue.

The guests were fully in it, and months later were still calling it one of the best weddings they had been to.

Your Wedding Can Start From Far Away Too

If you live abroad or in another part of Italy and want a wedding in Salento, Cristina and Matteo show that distance is not the obstacle — provided the team is right. Cala dei Balcani is set up to look after couples from anywhere, with a process that is tested, transparent and built to take the worry out of planning from far away.
